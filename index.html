Chuck Sweeney's Slides

For more than fifty years Chuck Sweeney kept a camera close at hand. He photographed the church he loved: the Chorale, winter retreats, Memorial Day breakfasts, Stockade and Pioneer Girls, and the friends he served beside. He photographed his family as it grew: birthdays and Christmas mornings, school plays and band concerts, softball and basketball games, graduations, weddings and anniversaries. And he photographed the road, from Yosemite and the Grand Canyon to Alaska, Hawaii, Mexico and Europe.

Through it all Pat was at his side, his faithful companion on every road trip and at every family celebration. Wherever they went she could usually be found with a book in her hand and one of their dogs close by.

After decades behind the camera, Chuck sat down with all 241 carousels, close to 30,000 slides. He captured every one and wrote notes for each wheel, so that his family, his church family and his friends could see them again: the faces we have loved, the places we shared, and the days we celebrated together, kept for the generations still to come.

A life given to others and to serving the Lord.
